summary: "The one who taught me a successful deploy is prepared long before the first `git push`."

## Context

First mission, team of about ten, legacy platform mid-migration to Kubernetes.
Him: 15 years in the trade, calm, never losing composure when a cluster went sideways.

## What he taught me

<Callout variant="tip" title="His signature line">
"Before you push, ask yourself: if I break my leg tonight, can my teammate roll back at 3am without me?"
</Callout>

The runbook before the feature. The rollback before the forward.
The blameless post-mortem. Pizza on the third incident in a row.

## What I kept

- **Document before deploying**: if it isn't written, it doesn't exist for whoever takes the on-call tomorrow.
- **Automate the rollback first**: a deploy isn't done until it can undo itself.
- **Calm spreads** as much as panic. Choose what you propagate.
