## What is collected

When you send a message through the contact form, the following is transmitted:

- your name,
- your email address,
- your company name, if you provide it,
- the subject and body of your message.

This goes straight to my inbox by email, through the delivery provider **Resend**.
It is not stored in any database, and it is never sold or passed to a third party
for commercial purposes.

## Why

Only to reply to you. No outreach, no newsletter, no profiling.

## How long

The message stays in my mailbox for as long as the exchange requires. You can ask
for its deletion at any time.

## Bot protection

The form is protected by **Cloudflare Turnstile**, a CAPTCHA alternative that does
not track visitors. Turnstile analyses browser behaviour to tell a human from a bot.
A per-IP submission limit is applied as well.

## Analytics and errors

- **Vercel Analytics** and **Speed Insights** measure traffic and performance
  without cookies or persistent identifiers.
- **Sentry** records technical errors so I can fix them. Email addresses and IP
  addresses are scrubbed before anything is stored.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, and object to the processing of your
data. To exercise it, write to contact@devcorporation.fr: requests are handled
within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection
authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr).
